# FitSphere AI 🏋️

**Your AI-powered fitness, nutrition, and wellness companion.**

FitSphere AI is a fitness application concept designed to help users track their nutrition, monitor workouts, discover personalized food suggestions, and stay motivated through social fitness features.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Build an all-in-one fitness platform that combines artificial intelligence, computer vision, nutrition tracking, and social engagement to support healthier habits.

## ✨ Planned Features

### 🍎 AI Food Scanner
- Upload a photo of a meal for food identification.
- Estimate calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.
- Allow users to correct food items and portion sizes.

### 💪 Smart Workout Tracker
- Use the camera to track supported exercises.
- Count repetitions using computer vision and pose estimation.
- Estimate calories burned during workouts.
- Track workout history and progress.

### 🥗 Personalized Food Suggestions
- Suggest meal ideas based on activity, time of day, and user preferences.
- Provide nutrition information to help users make informed choices.

### 🔥 Streaks and Notifications
- Track workout consistency and activity streaks.
- Send workout reminders and celebrate milestones.
- Encourage sustainable fitness habits.

### 👥 Social Fitness Community
- Share workout activities and progress.
- Follow friends and view their posts.
- Encourage and motivate other users.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology Stack

- **Mobile App:** React Native, Expo
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **ORM:** SQLAlchemy
- **Computer Vision:** OpenCV, MediaPipe
- **Machine Learning:** PyTorch
- **Version Control:** Git, GitHub

## 🚧 Project Status

**Planning and initial development.**

## 🔒 Privacy and Accuracy

- Protect users' personal information and fitness data.
- Treat food recognition, portion sizes, nutrition values, and calorie-burn estimates as approximations.
- Allow users to review and correct estimates.
- Provide supportive wellness guidance without replacing professional medical advice.

## 🌟 Vision

Make fitness more accessible, personalized, and motivating by bringing nutrition tracking, intelligent workout assistance, and social accountability together in one application.
